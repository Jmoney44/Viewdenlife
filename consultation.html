Before you reach out

A few notes can make the conversation easier.

If you are preparing to speak with a healthcare professional, you may find it useful to write down:

  • What you would like help understanding.
  • When the concern started and whether anything has changed.
  • Any medicines or supplements you currently use.
  • The questions you want answered before you leave.

Share only what is necessary for your enquiry. Avoid sending highly sensitive personal or medical information through general contact channels.
