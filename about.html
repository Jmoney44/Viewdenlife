A little about me

Stories start
with noticing.

The ordinary moments. The words left unsaid. The way a familiar place can hold a whole lifetime.

A writer, a wanderer,
an enthusiastic tea drinker.
Hello, I'm Viewdenlife

I'm glad
you're here.

I write about the moments that ask us to slow down: the long way home, the conversation that changes a friendship, the brave little step toward a life that fits.

When I'm not writing, you'll find me walking somewhere near the water, collecting books with beautiful covers, or trying to convince my garden to cooperate.

The Light We Keep is my first novel. It grew from a question I couldn't quite let go of: what if the stories we inherit aren't the whole story?
